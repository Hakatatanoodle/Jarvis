Knowledge

## KS-01 — Definition ✅ LOCKED

Knowledge is information that Jarvis can use but which is not inherently part of the user.

Examples:

- PDFs
- Books
- Notes
- GitHub repositories
- Emails
- Documentation
- Videos
- Research papers

Memory is about the user.

Knowledge is about the world.

---

## KS-02 — Knowledge Sources ✅ LOCKED

Knowledge comes from four sources.

- External
- Personal
- Generated
- Learned

---

## KS-03 — Knowledge Updates ✅ LOCKED

Knowledge is reference-based.

Jarvis stores references to knowledge rather than unnecessary copies.

Knowledge evolves through versioning.

Example:

Architecture v0.2

replaces

Architecture v0.1

History remains accessible.

---

## KS-04 — Freshness ✅ LOCKED

Knowledge should include:

- Source
- Timestamp
- Version

Knowledge may become outdated.

Jarvis should prefer fresher knowledge while preserving historical versions.

---

## KS-05 — Knowledge → Memory ✅ LOCKED

Knowledge may generate memories.

Example:

Reading dozens of AI papers over months

↓

Jarvis infers

"AI Systems has become one of Yochan's primary interests."

The paper remains knowledge.

The inferred interest becomes memory.

Knowledge is useful because it is current.

Older knowledge remains history.

---

## KS-06 — Knowledge Representation ✅ LOCKED

Knowledge is represented as a graph.

Relationships between knowledge are first-class.

Example:

```
Transformer Paper

↓

Attention

↓

Related To

↓

Jarvis Project
```
