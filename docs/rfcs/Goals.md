# Goals

Goals are hierarchical.

```
Mission
    ↓
Life Goals
    ↓
Projects
    ↓
Tasks
```

Mission is the root.

---

## G-02 — Goal Relationships ✅ LOCKED

Goals form a graph.

A goal may:

- Have one parent
- Have multiple children
- Support multiple parent goals

Example:

Exercise

supports

- Health
- Basketball
- Mental Wellbeing

---

## G-03 — Goal Versioning ✅ LOCKED

Goals evolve.

Never overwrite.

Archive previous versions.

Store why they changed.

---

## G-04 — Goal Lifecycle ✅ LOCKED

Mission

Permanent.

Life Goals

Long-term.

May complete or evolve.

Projects

Temporary.

Tasks

Temporary.

---

## G-05 — Goal Prioritization ✅ LOCKED

Priority is determined by a hybrid system.

Jarvis recommends priorities based on:

- Mission alignment
- Current goals
- Deadlines
- Dependencies
- Health
- Current mode
- Workload

User has final authority.

If a decision strongly conflicts with long-term goals, Jarvis should respectfully push back once.

---

## G-06 — Goal Success Metrics ✅ LOCKED

Every goal is evaluated using:

- Completion
- Consistency
- Results
